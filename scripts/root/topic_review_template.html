Do these topic groupings make sense?

Topics group rules by subject (Battle, Movement, Crafting…). They are the editor's choices, not part of the Law, so they need a second opinion before the site stops calling them drafts. Each card lists exactly what a topic collects and why.

  • Rules join a topic in three ways: a whole section of the Law, picked one by one with a reason, or a word in the rule's printed title (that's how every faction's own “Battle” action joins Battle).
  • Looks right if everything listed belongs and nothing important is missing. Problem if something is wrong or missing: say what in the note. Not sure is fine too.
  • Faction pages use these topics: a faction with its own rule on a topic gets that topic's core rules under “General rules that apply”. The last card asks about that.
  • When you're done, press Copy results and paste the text into the chat.
